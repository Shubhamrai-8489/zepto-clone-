KIS KAAM KE LIYE
----------------
Hum ek gig-partner onboarding app ke liye in-app help videos bana rahe hain:
onboarding ke har step pe us step ka chhota video dikhega, taaki partner
atke nahi. Do tarah ke layout soche gaye hain:
  A. Full-screen video, har step se pehle
  B. Video form ke upar chalta rahe, aur partner saath-saath form bhare
     (iske liye screen ka layout video ke hisaab se dobara banana hoga)
Yeh research usi ke liye reference hai: bade apps onboarding aur mobile
layout kaise karte hain, aur kya seekhne layak hai.

SABSE KAAM KI 6 BAATEIN
-----------------------
1. Pehle value dikhao, phir maango: dono apps bina login browse karne
   dete hain; login me sirf mobile number + OTP.
2. Ek screen, ek kaam: lambe form nahi. KYC ko chhote steps me todo.
3. Progress dikhao: Zepto rider ka hub — 3 steps, "10 min" ka vaada,
   locked steps — sabse achha example hai.
4. Wajah batao: Blinkit rider Aadhaar pe "5 min vs 1-2 din" dikha ke
   e-KYC ke liye manata hai, aur us screen pe help video bhi deta hai.
5. Mobile layout: header patla aur upar chipka hua, main button neeche
   chipka hua, beech ka hissa scroll. Layout B (form ke upar video) ke
   liye video ko upar ki sticky patti me rakhna isi se nikalta hai.
6. Galti rokne ke liye dikhao, likho mat: selfie ke sahi/galat photo
   examples text instructions se behtar kaam karte hain.
